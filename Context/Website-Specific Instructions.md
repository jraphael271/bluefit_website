## Relax Massage

**H1:** Relax Massage in Lent – Recovery & relaxation at Blue Fit

**Intro:** Do you suffer from muscle soreness, tension, or just want to fully relax? At Blue Fit Lent we work together with an experienced relaxation masseur who helps with recovery, relaxation, and injury prevention. So you feel fitter and recover faster after your workouts.

Our masseur receives you in his own practice in Lent, where you can come for professional, tailored treatments. We're happy to connect you with him so you can easily make an appointment.

> **Note:** The masseur works independently and outside of Blue Fit. Massages are booked and paid directly with him, but thanks to our partnership you can be sure you're in good hands.

**What to expect:** During a Relax Massage, our masseur focuses on your recovery. The massage stimulates circulation, removes waste products more quickly, and helps reduce muscle knots. Whether you exercise intensively or simply experience tension from work or stress — massage helps you move fluidly and energetically again.

**Benefits:**
- Promotes faster muscle recovery
- Prevents and reduces injuries
- Reduces muscle soreness and stiffness
- Improves circulation and mobility
- Relaxing for stress or tension

**Practical info:**
- Goal: muscle recovery, relaxation & injury prevention
- Duration: 60 min per session
- Guidance: certified masseur

### Relax Massage FAQ

**1. Who is relax massage suitable for?**
Not only for avid athletes. If you suffer from back, shoulder, or neck complaints from work, a massage can also provide relief.

**2. Does a relax massage hurt?**
It can sometimes be a bit tender, especially around muscle knots. But the masseur always tailors the intensity to your body.

**3. How often is a relax massage recommended?**
It depends on your training and workload. Avid athletes often choose a massage every 2–4 weeks. For complaints, more frequent sessions may be beneficial.

**4. Does relax massage help prevent injuries?**
Yes, it helps reduce tension, improve circulation, and thus prevent or speed up recovery from injuries.

**5. Can I book a relax massage at Blue Fit without being a member?**
Yes, relax massage in Lent is also available for non-members from Nijmegen and surroundings.

### Subscription FAQ

**1. Can I pause my subscription?**
Yes, you can temporarily suspend your membership with a flex subscription.

**2. What is the difference between Blue and Light Blue?**
With Light Blue you exercise during off-peak hours (10:00–16:00) and on weekends. Blue gives access from 06:00–23:00, 7 days a week.

**3. Can I try it out first?**
Yes, with Try Blue you exercise for a free week. Afterwards it automatically switches to a Blue subscription without cancellation. You can also take a day pass, which is credited when you become a member.

**4. Are group classes included in my subscription?**
Yes, all group classes are included with Blue and Flex. For special classes like Mama Flow and Bootcamp, separate group subscriptions are available.

**5. What does a day pass cost?**
A day pass costs €16.50. If you become a member afterwards, we credit the cost against your first payment.

**6. Can I get a subscription for only group classes or only fitness?**
No, when you're with us you have the opportunity to make use of all our facilities.

## About Us

Blue Fit was founded in 2025 with a clear mission: to create a gym where health goes beyond just training. Inspired by the principles of the Blue Zones — areas in the world where people live longer and healthier — we are building a place where movement, nutrition, purpose, and connection come together.

At Blue Fit we believe vitality comes from balance. Our four core values — **Movement, Healthy Nutrition, Purpose, and Community** — form the foundation of everything we do. From our training sessions and nutritional advice to the way we interact with each other.

We want to be more than a gym. Blue Fit is a community where people motivate, inspire, and support each other on the path to a healthy, happy, and meaningful life.

**Our mission:** Blue Fit wants to create the Blue Zone on the Waal — a place where people work together on a sustainable lifestyle. An environment where healthy living becomes self-evident, with attention to body, mind, and connection with others.

---

### Core Values

#### Movement
At Blue Fit we believe movement is the foundation of a vital life. Whether it's an intensive workout, a walk in nature, or a playful workout with others — movement gives energy, confidence, and balance. We encourage an active lifestyle in a way that suits you: sustainable and enjoyable.

#### Healthy Nutrition
Healthy nutrition is fuel for body and mind. We help you make conscious choices that aren't about strict rules, but about balance and enjoyment. With knowledge, inspiration, and practical tips, we make healthy eating achievable and fun — every day.

#### Purpose
Real vitality comes from within. Blue Fit goes beyond just being physically fit: it's also about purpose, growth, and doing what truly makes you happy. Together we discover what drives you, so you live with energy and meaning.

#### Community
At Blue Fit, we do it together. Our community is a place of connection, motivation, and support. We share successes, overcome challenges, and inspire each other to become a little fitter, stronger, and happier every day. Because together you go further.
