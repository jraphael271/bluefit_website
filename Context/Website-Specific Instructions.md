## Offer

### Fitness at Blue Fit

At Blue Fit in Lent, we believe fitness is more than just exercise. It's about sustainable movement, enjoyment, and working toward a strong and healthy body. Our fitness space is fully equipped with smart technology for strength and cardio. Our trainers are there to listen, guide, and motivate. We keep challenging you to set and achieve goals so you keep growing — physically and mentally. This way, everyone finds a way of training that suits their goals.

And we do it together. At Blue Fit, working out isn't a solo activity — it's something you share. Our community of Blue Fitters motivate each other, achieve results together, and enjoy the pleasure of exercising as a group.

#### Smart Equipment

Our smart devices make training easier, more varied, and more effective:

- See how each machine works via short instructional videos
- Train in different ways: standard, eccentric, isokinetic, or elastic
- Fine-tune the weight to your level and goal
- Whether you're a beginner or experienced athlete, this technology helps you get more out of every session

#### Cardio with Variety

Running, cycling, stair master, cross trainer, climbing, or rowing — our cardio park offers endless possibilities and more. Keep your fitness levels up and make every workout varied.

#### Personal Plan, Measurements and Evaluation

Upon registration, we always recommend making an appointment with a trainer to create a personal plan. Based on our smart scale and your wishes, we build a training schedule step by step. We'll explain every exercise so you can start the right way. Once your program is complete, we'll review your progress together and make any adjustments.

#### Strength Training & Hyrox

In our functional zone you'll find free weights, plate-loaded machines, cables, dumbbells, resistance bands, and much more. All the space you need to shape your training however you like. Also perfect if you want to prepare for events like Hyrox, where strength and endurance come together.

#### Blue Fit Community

At Blue Fit, you never train alone. Our community is there to motivate each other, train together, and celebrate successes. Because sport is more fun — and easier to stick with — when you do it together.

Come experience what fitness at Blue Fit feels like. Claim your free trial week and discover the benefits of our unique approach.

### Frequently Asked Questions — Fitness at Blue Fit

**1. Can I use the equipment without experience?**
Yes, every machine has a short instructional video and our trainers are always available to help. You can start safely and with confidence.

**2. What does a personal plan include?**
We always recommend planning a personal plan right away. We want to know how you live, whether there are injuries or other limitations, and most importantly what your goal is. From there we take measurements. You get insight into your body composition: weight, muscle mass, fat percentage, and hydration balance. Then we create a plan and explain it step by step.

**3. Are there free training options besides the machines?**
Yes, in addition to the smart machines we have a functional zone with free weights, plate-loaded machines, cables, and a sled, so you can train and vary freely.

**4. Can I train for HYROX or other events at Blue Fit?**
Absolutely! Our functional zone and cardio equipment are perfect for working toward HYROX or other sporting challenges.

**5. What if I have questions during my training?**
Our trainers and coaches are always on the floor during supervised hours to support you, improve your technique, and give tips.

**6. Can I try it out first?**
Yes, you can claim a free trial week to experience the fitness space, equipment, and atmosphere for yourself.

**7. Are there showers?**
Yes, multiple showers are available.

**8. Are there separate subscriptions or session cards?**
For a few specific classes (Mama Flow, Blue Stars and Blue Icons) there are separate subscriptions, but in general there are no separate subscriptions or session cards. There is a day pass.

**9. Can I work out here with Corporate Fitness?**
Yes, register via BFNL and we'll send you a welcome email once we receive your submission.

**10. Are there lockers with locks?**
Yes, these are available.

**11. Do you offer group classes on screen?**
No, every class we offer is live with an enthusiastic presenter.

**12. How do I log in?**
Via our website you can log in and access our (web) customer portal to book every class, plan your workouts, and much more.

**13. How do I get in?**
You can log in via our customer portal on your phone using your QR code if you're a member. Note: hold the QR code 20cm from the scanner.

**14. How can I cancel?**
You can cancel your subscription via email. We always appreciate hearing the reason, so we can improve ourselves if needed. Your subscription will then be cancelled with 28 days' notice, unless you're in an annual contract.

**15. What are your opening hours?**
We are open 365 days a year from 6:00 to 23:00.

**16. When is there supervised training?**
Supervision is available every morning Mon–Fri from 8:15–12:00 and 17:00–21:00, and on weekends from 8:30–12:00.

### Subscription FAQ

**1. Can I pause my subscription?**
Yes, you can temporarily suspend your membership with a flex subscription.

**2. What is the difference between Blue and Light Blue?**
With Light Blue you exercise during off-peak hours (10:00–16:00) and on weekends. Blue gives access from 06:00–23:00, 7 days a week.

**3. Can I try it out first?**
Yes, with Try Blue you exercise for a free week. Afterwards it automatically switches to a Blue subscription without cancellation. You can also take a day pass, which is credited when you become a member.

**4. Are group classes included in my subscription?**
Yes, all group classes are included with Blue and Flex. For special classes like Mama Flow and Bootcamp, separate group subscriptions are available.

**5. What does a day pass cost?**
A day pass costs €16.50. If you become a member afterwards, we credit the cost against your first payment.

**6. Can I get a subscription for only group classes or only fitness?**
No, when you're with us you have the opportunity to make use of all our facilities.

## About Us

Blue Fit was founded in 2025 with a clear mission: to create a gym where health goes beyond just training. Inspired by the principles of the Blue Zones — areas in the world where people live longer and healthier — we are building a place where movement, nutrition, purpose, and connection come together.

At Blue Fit we believe vitality comes from balance. Our four core values — **Movement, Healthy Nutrition, Purpose, and Community** — form the foundation of everything we do. From our training sessions and nutritional advice to the way we interact with each other.

We want to be more than a gym. Blue Fit is a community where people motivate, inspire, and support each other on the path to a healthy, happy, and meaningful life.

**Our mission:** Blue Fit wants to create the Blue Zone on the Waal — a place where people work together on a sustainable lifestyle. An environment where healthy living becomes self-evident, with attention to body, mind, and connection with others.

---

### Core Values

#### Movement
At Blue Fit we believe movement is the foundation of a vital life. Whether it's an intensive workout, a walk in nature, or a playful workout with others — movement gives energy, confidence, and balance. We encourage an active lifestyle in a way that suits you: sustainable and enjoyable.

#### Healthy Nutrition
Healthy nutrition is fuel for body and mind. We help you make conscious choices that aren't about strict rules, but about balance and enjoyment. With knowledge, inspiration, and practical tips, we make healthy eating achievable and fun — every day.

#### Purpose
Real vitality comes from within. Blue Fit goes beyond just being physically fit: it's also about purpose, growth, and doing what truly makes you happy. Together we discover what drives you, so you live with energy and meaning.

#### Community
At Blue Fit, we do it together. Our community is a place of connection, motivation, and support. We share successes, overcome challenges, and inspire each other to become a little fitter, stronger, and happier every day. Because together you go further.
